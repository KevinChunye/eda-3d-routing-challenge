## Title

Add kevin_toll_lns submissions (hard, congested, designs)

## Body

Method: C++20 router, from scratch, one config for all tiers.
1. PathFinder negotiated congestion with slow present-cost growth (×1.03 per round) and
   fanout-weighted costs (a k-sink net sees congestion costs × k^-0.3) until legal.
2. Exact per-net reroute: rip one net, route its true-delay shortest-path tree with all
   other nets fixed (optimal for that net), sweep until no improvement.
3. Large-neighbourhood search until the time limit: groups of up to 6 nets (a high-excess
   net plus the nets on its congestion-free tree, or the nets in a window around it, chosen by
   adaptive roulette), rerouted with a few negotiated rounds inside the group, kept if the
   group's delay does not rise.

Hardware: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz (4 vCPU), 16 GB. Threads: 1. One run per case,
`--jobs 1`, seed 1, time limit 60 s (hard) / 300 s (congested, designs).
